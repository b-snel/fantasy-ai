      How this decides

      Every trade is scored on both rosters: the points each team's best lineup is projected to score each week from now through the fantasy playoffs, with byes, injury returns, and waiver-wire streaming included. A trade is shown only if it helps your lineup, does not hurt theirs, and looks fair on paper by rest-of-season value, so they have a reason to say yes.
