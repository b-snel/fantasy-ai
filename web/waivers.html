    How this decides

    Each add is scored by what it does to your best legal lineup, week by week through the fantasy playoffs. The baseline is your roster with no further moves - no free streaming is assumed - so an empty slot scores nothing and a pickup that covers a bye gets full credit for that week.

    Weeks are not worth the same. Auto weights each one by how much a point that week moves your title odds, from a simulation of the rest of the season on the league's real schedule.

    A free agent can be added now; a player on waivers needs a claim that processes at the next run; a player whose game has started is locked until then. Suggested bids come from this league's own clearing prices and how many teams likely want him - a starting point, not a solver.
